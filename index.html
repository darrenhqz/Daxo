Case study
From 6-month delays to real-time close
A Singapore-based investment holding company. Six entities. Three currencies. One person running the entire finance function.

The situation

Management accounts running six months behind. External accountants dependent on late bank statements and manual data gathering. Every AP invoice manually entered. Month-end close spread across spreadsheets with no enforced procedure. The CFO had zero real-time visibility into the group's financial position.

6 entities USD / EUR / SGD Xero Cross-border Offshore maritime

What we built

A complete finance automation layer. PDF invoices parsed by AI and posted as draft bills in under two minutes. Month-end journals - depreciation, payroll, accruals, FX revaluation - fully automated. A 10-step close procedure enforced through a dashboard with period locking across all six entities.

The system handles edge cases that normally require senior accountant judgment: multi-currency invoices, GST detection on Singapore invoices, German informational VAT exclusion, and automatic accrual journal posting for invoices dated in locked periods.
